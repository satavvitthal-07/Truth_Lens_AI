Screening scope

  • MP4 containers only; files are validated before decoding.
  • Successfully analyzed videos are retained locally until you delete their history record or an administrator removes them.
  • At most 8 frames are sampled within the time limit.
  • Per-frame image scores are summarized without temporal analysis.
  • Audio, lip-sync and motion inconsistencies are not analyzed.
This is frame-based screening, not a dedicated video deepfake detector. Do not upload sensitive recordings; local storage is not a backup service.
